In realtasks, every regex that comes from the model or user (task_output grep, task_wait until, monitor filter, bash ready.pattern) goes through `userRegex(pattern, flags?)` in `src/text.ts`, which carries the `// nosemgrep: javascript.lang.security.audit.detect-non-literal-regexp...` suppression. Don't call `new RegExp(userInput)` directly: pi-lens's Semgrep flags it as a ReDoS warning. The codebase is also kept free of pi-lens ast-grep warnings (no nested ternaries; use `toReversed()` instead of `.reverse()`). Shared outcome text (`exit N` or the signal name) comes from `exitText()` in `src/text.ts`.
