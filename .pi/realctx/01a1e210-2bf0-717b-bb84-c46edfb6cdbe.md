In realtasks, the default `shortcuts.background` is `ctrl+b` (which Pi binds to `tui.editor.cursorLeft`). Instead of `setEditorComponent` or `registerShortcut` (which cannot decline a key), realtasks shares editor-only keys (`tui.editor.*`) via a raw `ctx.ui.onTerminalInput` listener (`backgroundInput` in `src/commands.ts`), consuming the key only when `ctx.ui.getEditorText()` is empty, at least one foreground command is running, and `rt.panelOpen` is false (neither `/tasks` nor `/tasks settings` is open), while ignoring key-release events. The foreground `bash` partial-result hint shows `"ctrl+b to background · auto in ..."` when `rt.backgroundKey` is active. Shortcut conflict planning and key-id validation live in `src/shortcuts.ts` (`planShortcut`, `keyIdProblem`, `shortcutProblem`); only `shortcuts.background` may share `tui.editor.*` bindings, while `shortcuts.manager` and any non-editor bindings clash.
